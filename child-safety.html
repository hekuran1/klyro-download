Klyro Child Safety Standards

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) policy · Last updated 17 November 2026 · Applies to the Klyro app (app.klyro.mobile) and its web pages, operated by KlyroApp.

1. Our standard

Klyro has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Klyro is a business tool for adult creators. It is not designed for, and must not be used by, anyone under 18; registration requires confirming you are at least 18.

2. What is prohibited

You may not use Klyro to create, upload, store, publish, sell, share, link to or promote:

  • sexual or sexualised content depicting or involving a minor, including AI-generated or altered content;
  • content that sexualises minors in any other way, or that advertises or seeks such material;
  • grooming, solicitation, sextortion, trafficking or any other exploitation of children;
  • anything that facilitates or encourages the above.

This applies to everything a user can put into Klyro, including product titles, descriptions, cover images, uploaded delivery files, profile pictures, video scripts, images and rendered videos, and public storefront pages.

3. How Klyro works, and what we can and cannot see

  • Klyro has no direct messaging between users and no public social feed. Content reaches other people mainly through a creator’s public storefront page and the download links emailed to that creator’s buyers.
  • We do not currently run automated detection (such as hash-matching or image classifiers) on uploads. We rely on reports, so reports matter.
  • Klyro is operated by KlyroApp and does not have a 24/7 moderation team.

4. How to report a concern

Email appklyro@gmail.com with the subject Child safety report. Please include, where you can, the link to the storefront, product or download page, the creator’s username, and what you saw. Do not send us or forward the illegal material itself.

This address is Klyro’s child safety contact. You can also report content that is illegal directly to the specialist bodies below; we encourage this:

  • United States: NCMEC CyberTipline
  • United Kingdom: Internet Watch Foundation
  • Elsewhere: a national hotline through INHOPE, or your local police

5. What we do when we receive a report

  • We review reports as quickly as we can. Because Klyro is a small operation we cannot promise a guaranteed response time, and we treat any report involving a child as the highest priority.
  • If content or an account breaches this policy, we remove the content, and we remove the creator’s storefront and account. Violating accounts are not allowed back.
  • Where we become aware of apparent CSAM or the exploitation of a child, we will preserve the relevant information as the law requires and report it to the appropriate authority, such as NCMEC or law enforcement in the relevant country. We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.
  • We do not tell the reported user who made a report, and we do not publish details of reports.

6. Users under 18

If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact us at the address above and we will delete it. Anyone can also delete their own account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or at klyro-api.onrender.com/account-deletion.
